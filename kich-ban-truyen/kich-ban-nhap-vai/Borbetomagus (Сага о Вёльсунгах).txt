#	Game Deeds
The trier can be a wight or fellowship.
There are 2 ways of choosing outcomes of game deeds: a deed trial, or deed tracking.
All of the ways need a roll of dice for luck.
A higher Luck score is better for the trier.

##	Boons And Banes
Sum numbers for boons and banes with the luck score of the trier.
Things which help the trier are boons and make the score of the trier greater;
a boon is summed as a score greater than nought.
Things which hinder the trier are banes and dwindle the score of the trier;
a bane is summed as a score less than nought.

##	Deed Tracking
If a deed is sure to get done in the end, then when is done can be judged by Deed Tracking,
otherwise use a Deed Trial for winning or losing.
Headway in a Deed can be measured by Deed Tally.
The Deed Tally shows what is still left to do to finish the deed.
Deed Tally dwindles while working on the Deed.
The starting Deed Tally can not be more than 120.
Unless it is in a fight round for which Luck has already been chosen, 1D12 is rolled for Luck.
Each step until the Deed is done, Deed Tally for a Deed dwindles by Luck + Thew + Skill.
Not much else can be done while doing the Deed.
When there are no more Deed Tally, the Deed is done.
For a given deed, there is a minimum time span which must pass before the next step.

##	Deed Trial
A deed which either happens or does not can be judged by a Deed Trial.
Scores of triers are meted against a hurdle mark or scores of rivals.
Whether the trier wins or loses, and how good or bad the outcome is for him,
is chosen by how much greater or less his score is than the hurdle mark.
The height of the hurdle mark can be set by Pure Luck xor Scores of Rivals xor Ease of the Deed.

###	Deed Trial By Pure Luck
Deciding whether a deed happens by pure luck, choose a Luck die with a number of sides suitable and a number as hurdle which the die must reach for the trier win.
The Luck die can have from 2 through 32766 sides.

###	Deed Trial by Ease of The Deed
The trier's score must be as high or higher than the hurdle mark to win.
