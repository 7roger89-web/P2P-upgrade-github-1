## Vertical findings

This is the most scientifically interesting repo in the set because it published a prediction-validation experiment rather than only successful anecdotes.

Its own final report tested 100 Polymarket binary questions, with 79 resolved. The Meihua agent scored **40/79 = 50.6%**, while the control scored **57/79 = 72.2%** and market consensus about **73.4%**. Reported permutation p-values did not support an advantage for the Meihua procedure. A more elaborate reinterpretation using additional hexagram relations scored about **48.1%**, also not useful in that experiment. High-confidence calls were not calibrated.

The report also acknowledges design problems, especially a phase where one hexagram was reused across many questions, conflicting with 一事一占. Therefore the correct conclusion is narrow: **the tested operationalization did not show predictive value**. It is not a universal disproof of every historical or modern Mai Hoa practice.
