## Vertical findings

The strongest part is case/product architecture, not validation. `apps/backend/src/models/case.js` stores question, original/mutual/final hexagrams, moving line, time, prediction, result, correctness, rethink and external response. This is useful for a research notebook.

However `case.service.js` supports direct row update (`amendCase`) and the model does not expose an immutable prediction version/hash. A prediction can therefore be edited after an outcome unless the application layer imposes controls elsewhere. This is inadequate as blind-test evidence.

The README says 梅花, 策轨, 六爻纳甲 and 神煞 coexist in the product. That is a product claim, not proof that the methods form one coherent doctrine.

## Three-axis verdict

- `ENGINEERING`: B — buildable product, no real automated test suite.
- `PROVENANCE`: C — mixed-method content needs source-by-source audit.
- `PREDICTIVE`: UNPROVEN — mutable case records cannot establish blind predictive validity.
