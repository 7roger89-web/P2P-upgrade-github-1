## 1. Purpose and authority

This ledger records, for every researched external source, Owner idea, or internal research direction: what was researched, what was **selected**, what was deliberately **rejected**, how selected ideas translate into provider-neutral P2P capabilities, what has been implemented/shipped/verified, what remains planned/partial/deferred, and where the evidence lives.
