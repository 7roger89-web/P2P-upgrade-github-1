# ROUND 2 — 02 PROMPT_RESEARCHER — disagreements / risks / corrections

READ: R1 01,03,04,05,06,07.

D1 — vs 01. MATERIAL DISAGREEMENT on prompt length, and a caveat on structure.
01's full assembly (reference declaration + premise + global setting + timeline + global tail with
two-layer bans) is far longer than anything in the with-prompt subset of my corpus. I cannot
evidence that long two-layer negative blocks help; I *can* evidence that short, single-idea
prompts are what shipped. I accept the fixed tail as a continuity device but flag it as
unevidenced by my source, and I object to long negative lists inside the body.

D2 — vs 06. CORRECTION OF SCOPE — important. 06 grounds the plan in Wan2.2 (an open local model),
while my corpus and 01's source are Seedance 2.5 (a hosted product). These are two different
prompt surfaces with different reference-slot semantics. The plan must not pretend one prompt text
serves both. Recommendation: author the Seedance-shaped prompt as primary (that is what the
brief's ARENA/DESIGNARENA surfaces resemble) and treat Wan2.2 wording as a separate variant.

D3 — vs 03. AGREEMENT, and my corpus independently supports it: verbatim clause reuse across
prompts is observably what consistent series do. 03's "reference carries the person, prompt carries
the beat" is the cleaner statement of it.

D4 — vs 04/05. AGREEMENT. Neither re-camera nor reference-editing appears as a prerequisite in any
posted workflow I can see; both are second passes. Keeping them out of the critical path matches
observed practice.

D5 — vs 07. NEUTRAL, one correction. 07 asks for no baked text — my corpus agrees strongly; posted
clips with baked captions are the minority and read as lower production value.

RISK I OWN: survivorship bias. Everything I contribute describes posted successes. The plan should
budget re-rolls per shot rather than assuming first-pass success. Recommend an explicit re-roll
budget line in the plan.

CORRECTION TO MY OWN R1: I said "keep each shot independently postable." With 03's continuity spine
that is still true, but it must not license re-prompting a shot from scratch. Independently
*postable*, not independently *authored*.
