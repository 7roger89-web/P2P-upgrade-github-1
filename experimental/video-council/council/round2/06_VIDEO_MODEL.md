# ROUND 2 — 06 VIDEO_MODEL — disagreements / risks / corrections

READ: R1 01,02,03,04,05,07.

D1 — vs 02. MATERIAL DISAGREEMENT — the most consequential open question in this council.
02 proposes authoring for the hosted Seedance surface as primary and treating Wan2.2 as a variant.
I accept the *prompt-surface* split but reject any implication that the technical envelope is
negotiable per surface. The keyframes are a single asset bundle; they must be authored once, at
one resolution and aspect, or the two surfaces diverge irreversibly. So: two prompt variants,
ONE 1280×720 / 16:9 / 24fps asset envelope.

D2 — vs 01. ACCEPTED with a clarification. 01 is right that duration and aspect do not go in the
Seedance prompt text. That is a prompt-surface rule, not a planning rule; the plan still fixes the
envelope, and for a local Wan2.2 path resolution/frames are CLI arguments, not prompt text either.
No conflict once stated that way.

D3 — vs 03. AGREEMENT, and I want it escalated to a hard plan rule: **no shot is authored T2V.**
Every shot begins from a locked keyframe (I2V/TI2V path, or an image-referenced hosted call).
Without that, the backbone has no identity lock and a xianxia face will drift by shot 3.

D4 — vs 05. AGREEMENT, and I confirm the mismatch from my side: Wan2.2 is a different generation
from the Wan2.1 base VACE ships against. Any future combined pipeline is two environments, not one.
Record it as a named risk.

D5 — vs 04. AGREEMENT. Camera authored in-prompt; no trajectory control from my side either.

D6 — vs 07. CORRECTION. 07 suggests ~0.5s handles per clip. On the open path, clip length is set in
frames (e.g. 24fps × 5s = 120 frames); asking for handles means authoring ~144 frames and trimming.
That is fine but must be stated in frames, not vaguely in seconds, or the delivered clips will be
short of usable material.

RISK I OWN: someone reading my R1 may treat "TI2V-5B runs on a 4090" as permission to download
weights. It is not. Wan2.2 is reference-only in this task; any render requires a separate,
explicitly authorised task.
