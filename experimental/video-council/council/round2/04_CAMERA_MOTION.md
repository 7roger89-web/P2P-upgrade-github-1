# ROUND 2 — 04 CAMERA_MOTION — disagreements / risks / corrections

READ: R1 01,02,03,05,06,07.

D1 — vs 01. MINOR CORRECTION on the camera ladder. 01 assigns a slow crane-up to S4. A crane-up on
the final shot changes the horizon relationship to the city and, combined with a walking subject,
is a two-variable move. Recommend S4 = slow push-in or locked-off wide with the subject walking
away. Keep the crane as an optional alternate, not the default.

D2 — vs 05. MATERIAL DISAGREEMENT on ordering, low stakes but worth recording. 05's Expand-Anything
argues for loose framing so the frame can be widened later; my lane argues for headroom so a
trajectory has room. Same request, different reasons — but the two together risk everything being
framed too loosely and the hero becoming small. Cap it: compose normally, reserve ~10% margin,
do not compose for hypothetical later passes.

D3 — vs 06. AGREEMENT and a useful constraint I adopt: trajectory control is not a native text
control, so camera phrasing must stay plain-language. I withdraw any implication that numeric
trajectories belong anywhere in this production.

D4 — vs 03. AGREEMENT, adopted as a hard rule: no horizontal flips at any stage, including in edit.
I will add that no shot may be reversed in post either — 07 should know this.

D5 — vs 07. AGREEMENT. "End shots on settled framing" is compatible with one-move-per-shot; I'd
strengthen it: every camera move decelerates to rest before the cut.

D6 — vs 02. NEUTRAL. Plain camera shorthand from the corpus is exactly the vocabulary I want.

RISK I OWN: scope creep back toward ReCamMaster. It is genuinely attractive for getting an
alternate angle on S3 without re-rolling the character, and someone will propose it. The plan must
mark it clearly as OUT for this production, gated behind a separate authorised task with GPU and
Wan2.1 weights, and with the authors' own caveat that the open version underperforms the paper.
