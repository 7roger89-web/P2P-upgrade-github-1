# ROUND 2 — 03 CHARACTER_CONSISTENCY — disagreements / risks / corrections

READ: R1 01,02,04,05,06,07.

D1 — vs 01. MATERIAL DISAGREEMENT (now largely resolved by 01's R2 D2, restated for the record).
01's R1 asked for two separately generated character cards plus a prop card. Two independent
generations of "the same" character is precisely the drift mechanism my source names. Required
correction: ONE canonical identity image; every other view is derived from it. The prop card must
also be derived, not independently invented, because the golden jian is identity-bearing.

D2 — vs 06. STRONG AGREEMENT, and it strengthens my position architecturally. 06 states the
backbone provides no identity lock and that I2V inherits identity from the input frame far more
reliably than T2V invents it. That converts "keyframe-first" from a stylistic preference into a
structural requirement. If any shot is authored T2V, identity is unlocked and the plan fails.

D3 — vs 04. AGREEMENT plus a hard constraint I want in the plan: no mirroring, ever. 04's
"do not cross the axis" and my "flipping swaps the sword hip and the collar cross" are the same
failure. Screen direction left→right, sword on the left hip, collar crossing right-over-left,
in all four shots and in the storyboard sheet.

D4 — vs 05. PARTIAL DISAGREEMENT. 05 frames MV2V masked repair as the continuity safety net. It is
banned runtime here, so the plan must not lean on it. Also, masked repair regenerates pixels inside
the mask — identity can shift there too. The real safety net is the canonical reference, not a
repair tool.

D5 — vs 02. CAUTION. "Re-specify wardrobe in text every time" is right, but only as a *frozen
token list* copied verbatim. Re-describing the character in fresh words each shot is drift by
another name. The distinction must be explicit in the plan.

D6 — vs 07. AGREEMENT. The cover frame must be taken from an existing locked shot, not generated
separately — a separately generated cover is a fifth character generation.

RISK I OWN: verification. Nothing in this plan currently *checks* identity. Add a manual gate —
compare every generated still against the canonical image, never against the previous shot.
