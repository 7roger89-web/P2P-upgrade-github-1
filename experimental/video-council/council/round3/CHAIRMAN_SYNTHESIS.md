never in prompt text. Both experts agreed once stated this way.

against unchanged canonical references. VACE MV2V masked repair is a future upgrade, not a
dependency.

R-06 Framing looseness (04 vs 05). RULING: compose normally with ~10% margin. Do not compose for
out-of-scope future passes. 05 withdrew.

R-07 Camera on S4 (04 vs 01). RULING: 04's correction accepted. S4 = slow push-in, locked horizon.
Crane-up recorded as an optional alternate only.

R-08 Handles (07 vs 01 vs 06). RULING: specified in frames. Author 144 frames per shot, cut to 120.

R-09 Mirroring. RULING: unanimous hard rule. No horizontal flip, no clip reversal, anywhere,
including in the edit.

R-10 T2V. RULING: 06 + 03 escalated correctly. No shot may be authored text-only. Every shot starts
from a locked keyframe.
