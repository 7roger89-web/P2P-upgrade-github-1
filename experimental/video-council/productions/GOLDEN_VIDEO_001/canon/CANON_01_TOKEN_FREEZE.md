# CANON_01 — IMMUTABLE TOKEN FREEZE
CHARACTER_ID=CANON_01 · PRODUCTION=GOLDEN_VIDEO_001
Authoritative identity list. Copy these tokens **verbatim** into every later keyframe and video
prompt. Never paraphrase them — re-describing the character in fresh words is drift.
Supersedes the provisional token list in PRODUCTION_PLAN.md §1.

## IMMUTABLE_IDENTITY
> young adult male xianxia cultivator, 22–26; oval face, high cheekbones, defined jaw;
> almond danfeng eyes with dark amber-brown irises; straight-swept dark eyebrows;
> narrow straight nose; medium-thin closed lips; fair warm-toned matte skin; no scars, no facial hair

## IMMUTABLE_HAIR
> long near-black glossy hair to mid-back, half-up in a smooth topknot held by one slim pale-jade
> ring, two thin face-framing strands

## IMMUTABLE_BODY
> tall slim athletic build, eight heads tall, moderate shoulders, narrow waist, long clean limbs,
> elegant long-fingered hands, upright posture

## IMMUTABLE_COSTUME
> three-layer ivory-white cultivation robe (inner under-robe, floor-length crossed-front main robe,
> sheer pale over-robe); collar crossing RIGHT over LEFT; wide long sleeves; gold-thread
> cloud-scroll embroidery on cuffs and lower hem only; woven gold belt with small centre clasp and
> two ivory sash ties on the left; low white cloth boots; light silk in long vertical folds

## IMMUTABLE_WEAPON
> one straight double-edged golden jian; blade ≈ shoulder-to-floor length with a shallow central
> fuller; small plain flat gold crescent guard; charcoal cord diamond-wrapped grip over gold;
> plain round gold pommel with one short pale tassel; faint steady warm-gold inner glow along the
> fuller and edges; scabbard on the LEFT hip

## IMMUTABLE_STYLE
> high-end cinematic xianxia key art; photoreal materials with a slight painterly finish;
> silk as silk, gold as brushed metal, blade as polished steel; high texture detail;
> restrained warm-neutral grade

- **Face identity** — the six identity tokens above. The danfeng eye shape, the straight-swept
  brows and the narrow straight nose bridge are the primary anchors.
- **Hair identity** — long, mid-back, near-black, half-up topknot with the single jade ring and the
  two face-framing strands. Never cut, recoloured, fully bound or restyled.
- **Body proportions** — eight heads tall, slim athletic, moderate shoulders, long limbs.
  Never bulked up, shortened or made adolescent.
- **Costume design** — white-and-gold three-layer robe, collar RIGHT over LEFT, gold only on cuffs,
  hem and belt. No armour, cape, hood, jewellery, colour change or collar flip.
- **Sword design** — one straight golden jian, the exact guard, grip, pommel and single tassel,
  blade ≈ half the character's height, scabbard on the LEFT hip. No second sword, no curved blade,
  no runes or gems, no size change.
- **No horizontal mirroring** of any image at any stage — a flip reverses the collar and moves the
  sword to the wrong hip.
- **No independent re-generation** of the character. All later material derives from CANON-01 or
  from an approved storyboard/keyframe derived from CANON-01.
