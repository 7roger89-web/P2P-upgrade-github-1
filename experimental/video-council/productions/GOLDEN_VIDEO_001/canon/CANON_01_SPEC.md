## 1. IDENTITY
- **Apparent age range:** 22–26. Young adult; adult jaw, no adolescent softness, no middle age.
- **Facial structure:** oval face tapering to a defined but not sharp chin; high flat cheekbones;
  clean jawline; smooth forehead; balanced symmetry; slim face, not gaunt.
- **Eye characteristics:** almond-shaped, slightly upswept outer corners (danfeng), dark amber-brown
  irises, clear whites, calm steady gaze, upper lids with a subtle single fold.
- **Eyebrows:** straight-swept, medium thickness, dark, angled slightly upward toward the temples;
  clean unbroken arch, no gaps, no scar breaks.
- **Nose:** straight narrow bridge, refined tip, modest nostrils, in line with the face's centre.
- **Lips:** medium-thin, softly defined cupid's bow, neutral rose tone, closed.
- **Skin appearance:** fair with a warm undertone, even, fine-pored, healthy matte with a light
  natural sheen on the cheekbones and nose bridge. No heavy makeup, no blemishes, no scars.
- **Defining facial features (the identity anchors):** the danfeng eye shape, the straight-swept
  brows, and the narrow straight nose bridge. These three read at any distance and are what a
  reviewer checks first.
- **Expression baseline:** composed and quietly confident. Lips closed and level, brows relaxed,
  eyes direct. Not smiling, not scowling, not blank.

## 2. HAIR
- **Length:** long — reaches mid-back when down.
- **Hairstyle:** half-up. The upper section is gathered into a smooth topknot held by a slim
  pale-jade ring; the remainder falls loose down the back, with two thin face-framing strands.
- **Hairline:** natural, even, no widow's peak, no receding corners, forehead partly visible.
- **Texture:** straight to very slightly wavy, fine, glossy, dark near-black with a cool sheen
  under key light. Individual strands readable, not a solid mass.
- **Ornaments:** exactly one — the slim pale-jade ring at the topknot. No crowns, no hairpins,
  no beads, no ribbons, no headband.
- **What must never change:** the length (long, mid-back), the half-up topknot with the single
  jade ring, the near-black colour, the two face-framing strands. Hair may move with wind or
  motion; it may never be cut, tied differently, recoloured, or fully bound.

## 3. BODY
- **Height impression:** tall, roughly 8 heads tall; reads as above-average height for the world.
- **Shoulder width:** moderate — about 2.2 head-widths. Defined, not broad or bulky.
- **Build:** slim athletic. Lean muscle, flat torso, narrow waist. Swordsman, not a bodybuilder.
- **Limb proportions:** long clean limbs, elegant elongated hands with visible tendon definition,
  fingers slender and correctly numbered.
- **Posture:** upright, weight settled evenly, chin level, shoulders relaxed and back.
  Composed stillness, not tension.
- **Silhouette:** a tall narrow vertical column — head, topknot, straight shoulders, long robe
  falling to the boots — broken only by the sword line. The silhouette must read at thumbnail size.

## 4. COSTUME
- **Garment layers (inner → outer), three layers:**
  1. inner white under-robe, high crossed collar, visible at the throat and cuffs;
  2. main white cultivation robe, crossed front, floor-length, wide sleeves;
  3. a sheer pale outer over-robe hanging from the shoulders, slightly longer than the main robe.
- **Dominant colours:** ivory-white and soft off-white.
- **Secondary colours:** pale warm grey in the shadow folds; a thin dark charcoal edge line on the
  main robe's collar band.
- **Gold ornamentation:** restrained and specific — a woven gold belt band; gold-thread cloud-scroll
  embroidery on the cuffs and the lower hem only; a small gold clasp at the centre of the belt.
  No gold plating, no gold armour panels, no all-over gold pattern.
- **Collar:** crossed, **right-over-left** as seen on the character's body. This is a hard identity
  token and the most common thing an image model flips.
- **Sleeves:** wide and long, falling past the fingertips when the arms hang; gold cloud-scroll
  border at the cuff.
- **Belt:** woven gold band at the natural waist, small gold clasp centred, two narrow ivory sash
  ties hanging from it down the left side.
- **Boots:** low white cloth boots with dark charcoal soles, mostly hidden under the hem.
- **Fabric behavior:** light silk. Falls in long vertical folds, drifts and settles slowly, catches
  light softly. It flows; it does not billow like a cape or stiffen like leather.
- **Specific visual motifs:** gold cloud-scroll embroidery (cuffs + hem), the woven gold belt with
  its centre clasp, the sheer pale over-robe.
- **Forbidden costume changes:** no armour plates, pauldrons, breastplate or bracers; no cape or
  hood; no colour change away from white-and-gold; no collar flip to left-over-right; no added
  jewellery, amulets, talismans or beads; no bare chest or open robe; no modern garments.

## 5. WEAPON
One canonical golden sword — a straight double-edged Chinese **jian**. There is exactly one.
- **Blade proportions:** blade length roughly equal to the distance from the character's shoulder
  to the floor — about 90 cm — and about 4 cm wide at the guard, tapering evenly to the point.
  Total sword length is about 1/2 the character's height.
- **Blade shape:** straight, double-edged, symmetrical, with a shallow central fuller running most
  of its length, tapering to a clean point. No curve, no serration, no fantasy silhouette.
- **Guard:** a small flat gold guard, shaped like a shallow downturned crescent, about 9 cm wide —
  wider than the grip but narrow relative to the blade. Plain, unornamented.
- **Hilt:** straight grip, about one hand-and-a-half long, wrapped in dark charcoal cord in a tight
  diamond weave over gold underlay.
- **Pommel:** a plain rounded gold disc with a short pale tassel. One tassel, always.
- **Glow behavior:** a faint warm-gold inner glow along the fuller and the edges only — as if the
  metal itself is lit from within. Steady, low intensity. It casts a soft warm light on the nearest
  hand and sleeve. It is **not** flame, lightning, particles, energy trails, aura, or a light beam,
  and it must never bloom enough to hide the blade's geometry.
- **Scale relative to body:** blade ≈ shoulder-to-floor height; the whole sword ≈ half the
  character's height. If the sword reads longer than the character is tall, it is wrong.
- **Canonical side / orientation:** the sword's **scabbard hangs on the LEFT hip**. In CANON-01
  the sword is **drawn and held in the right hand, point angled down toward the ground at
  roughly 30°, blade fully visible along its length, not crossing the body or the face.**
  This orientation is canonical; the image must never be horizontally mirrored, as a flip moves
  the sword to the wrong hip and reverses the collar.
- **Forbidden redesigns:** no second sword; no curved blade, sabre or katana; no oversized
  greatsword; no glowing runes, engraved script or gemstones; no floating or levitating sword;
  no chain, no guard wings, no crossguard quillons; no change to the tassel count or the
  charcoal-over-gold grip wrap.

## 6. STYLE
- **Rendering style:** high-end cinematic xianxia key art. Photoreal proportions and materials
  with a slight painterly finish. Not anime, not cel-shaded, not 3D-cartoon, not oil painting.
- **Realism level:** realistic anatomy, skin and cloth physics; idealised but believable features.
- **Cinematic qualities:** shallow-to-moderate depth of field, clean subject separation from the
  background, filmic contrast, restrained warm-neutral colour grade.
- **Lighting intent:** soft three-quarter key from the front-left at head height, gentle fill on the
  right, a subtle cool rim along the right edge to lift the silhouette. Even and diagnostic — the
  face and every costume detail must be legible. No dramatic chiaroscuro, no coloured gels, no
  backlight silhouetting, no lens flare.
- **Material treatment:** silk reads as silk (soft sheen, fine weave); gold reads as brushed metal,
  not plastic or chrome; the blade reads as polished steel with a warm internal light; skin is matte
  with subtle subsurface warmth.
- **Texture quality:** high — visible fabric weave, embroidery thread, individual hair strands,
  cord wrap on the grip. Sharp on the face and the sword.
- **Xianxia visual language:** immortal-cultivator idiom — flowing white silk, restrained gold,
  jade, long hair, the jian. Elegant and clean, never armoured or barbaric, never cluttered.

## 7. CANONICAL CAMERA
- **Body framing:** **full body**, head to boots, with clear margin above the topknot and below the
  boots. Nothing cropped — not the head, not the sword point, not the hem.
- **Camera height:** chest height, level. No low hero angle, no high angle.
- **View angle:** front three-quarter, turned about 20° off axis toward the camera's left, with the
  face turned back to near-frontal so both eyes are fully visible.
- **Lens feel:** 85mm portrait feel — flat, undistorted, natural proportions.
- **Visibility requirement:** the frame must let a reviewer inspect, in one image, the face, the
  full body proportions, the robe construction (collar, belt, sleeves, hem, layers) and the entire
  sword from point to pommel.
- **Aspect:** 2:3 portrait, high resolution. Identity plate only — shot keyframes remain 16:9.

## 8. BACKGROUND
Flat neutral mid-grey seamless studio backdrop with a very soft even gradient. No environment,
no city, no mountains, no mist, no props, no floor pattern, no text, no logo, no other figure.
This image is for identity locking, not for showing the fantasy city.

## 9. POSE
Neutral heroic standing pose. Facing front three-quarter, weight settled on both feet slightly
apart, spine upright, chin level, gaze to camera. Left arm hangs relaxed at the side with the
sleeve falling naturally. Right arm is lowered and slightly away from the body, holding the drawn
jian point-down at about 30°, the blade clear of the robe and clear of the face.
The pose must expose: the whole face, the full body proportions, the robe's construction from
collar to hem, and the complete sword.
Avoid extreme action posing — no leaping, flying, mid-swing, crouching, back turned, dynamic
foreshortening, or wind-blasted hair covering the face.
